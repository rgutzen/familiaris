# Contributing

Thank you for considering a contribution. Two things to know first.

**This public repository is generated.** The canonical tree is private, and
each release here is exported from it. A pull request can't be merged as-is: an
accepted change is applied to the private tree by hand and appears in the next
release, credited to you. Issues, bug reports and ideas are the easiest way to
contribute.

**Contributions need the agreement below.** Familiaris is dual-licensed: under
the GNU AGPL-3.0 for everyone, and under a commercial licence for those who
need one (see `NOTICE`). Offering both is only possible if the author can
license every part of the code. The agreement below gives that permission. You
keep the copyright in your contribution.

To agree, add this line to your pull request or issue, with your name:

> I have read the Contributor Licence Agreement in CONTRIBUTING.md and agree to
> it for this and my future contributions to Familiaris. — *Name*
