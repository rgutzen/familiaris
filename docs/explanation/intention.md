# Intention — what this is for

Every mechanism in this tree answers a question someone asked about their own
working life, and each mechanism is described somewhere else. This page is the
one place the purpose is stated, because a purpose repeated on every page
dilutes and a purpose stated nowhere is lost.

## The problem

A mind holds a subjective reality far richer than language can carry. Compressing
it into linear symbols is lossy — necessarily, and unevenly — and what is lost in
the compression is where isolation between people begins. Tools that ask for the
summary get the summary and lose the rest.

## The aim

To build pathways — technological, artistic, communal — that let one person's
first-person world be reached and felt by another. Not uniformity, and not a
merging of minds: a collaborative multiplicity of beings, each autonomous, each
able to share deeply with the others.

This repository is one of those pathways, and only the part of it that manages
text: it keeps a person's own record in their own words, in a format that
outlives the tools reading it, so that the record can be returned to — by its
author first, and by anyone else the author chooses.

## What that asks of a memory system

| The requirement | Where the mechanism is |
|---|---|
| the record must outlive the session that made it, while the session itself is cheap to lose | [`../../memory/knowledge/system/knowledge/memory-architecture.md`](../../memory/knowledge/system/knowledge/memory-architecture.md) |
| what is kept must be *re-acquirable*, so storage is priced by what cannot be got again rather than by size | [`../../STORAGE-MODEL.md`](../../STORAGE-MODEL.md) |
| a model's claim becomes the system's belief only through a human-gated promotion | [`../../memory/knowledge/system/consolidation/memory-poisoning-posture.md`](../../memory/knowledge/system/consolidation/memory-poisoning-posture.md) |

The third row follows from the aim rather than from engineering convenience. A
system that gathers a person's inner life on their behalf is worth exactly as
much as its refusal to let anything else write there.

## What it does not claim

**Not a hive mind.** Diverse, autonomous beings sharing deeply is the aim;
merging or uniform thinking is its opposite. Any framing that implies the second
misreads the first.

**Not the software.** The pathways are technological, artistic and communal. This
repository is one, its scope is text, and the parts of a life it cannot hold are
most of them.
